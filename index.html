01 / ART PROJECT

NIGHT VISION

2022 / 40 WORKS

An exploration of the boundary between light and darkness, the visible and the hidden.

The project emerged from experiments with light, shadow, colour and perception during a period of rapid development in digital art and NFTs.

Night Vision is more than a series of images. It is a visual archive of emotional states captured in time.

Revisiting these works years later becomes a continuation of the project itself, allowing an unchanged image to acquire new meanings.

03 / ABOUT

SOLDVER
PRODUCTION

SOLDVER PRODUCTION is an independent creative platform exploring the relationship between image, memory, time and perception.

Working at the intersection of photography, digital art, music and conceptual storytelling, the platform creates a space for artistic experimentation.

An image is not an endpoint, but the beginning of a dialogue. Its meaning evolves with the viewer, the artist and the passage of time.

Art does not have to provide answers. It can leave room for interpretation.

ABOUT THE ARTIST

Artem Solovyov (SOLDVER) is a Russian digital artist and creator of conceptual visual projects, working across photography, digital art and music.

In 2022, he created Night Vision, a series of 40 works that laid the foundation for his artistic language.

Today, SOLDVER revisits his early archive, reinterpreting his work through the lens of time, memory and personal transformation.

SOLDVER PRODUCTION serves as an independent space for continuing these explorations and developing new artistic projects.
