01 / JANUARY 2022

The beginning

SOLDVER began in January 2022 in Svetlogorsk. Inspired by the cinematography and soundtrack of Adam Randall’s film Night Teeth, Artem Solovyov began exploring the relationship between image, sound and the atmosphere of the city after dark.

02 / 2022–2023

Night Vision

These experiments developed into Night Vision, a limited collection of visual works released in episodes. The first works were presented on Mintable in February 2022. The early creative work was carried out using a single smartphone, a Xiaomi Redmi 7.

03 / JULY 2022

Image and sound

In July 2022, a work from Night Vision became part of the cover artwork for Pazilat Omarova’s song Derkhab. Under the name Soldver PR, the artist helped bring the recording to digital music platforms. The song gained popularity organically over the following years and appeared in social media trends in 2024.

04 / 2024

Beyond the image

The creative exploration expanded into cinema, including an independent artistic interpretation of Tim Burton’s Corpse Bride and the development of original visual concepts. SOLDVER PRODUCTION continues to explore the relationship between image, memory, time and perception.
