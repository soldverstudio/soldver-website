01

Artist profile

SOLDVER is an independent creative project established in January 2022 in Svetlogorsk. Inspired by the cinematography and soundtrack of Adam Randall’s Night Teeth, the artist began exploring the relationship between image, sound and the city after dark.

02

Creative philosophy

SOLDVER PRODUCTION explores image, memory, time and perception through photography, digital art, music and conceptual storytelling. Art can leave room for interpretation.

03

Night Vision / 2022–2023

A limited collection of visual works released in episodes. The first works were presented on Mintable in February 2022. Early creative work before 2024 was carried out using a Xiaomi Redmi 7.

04

Derkhab / 2022

A work from Night Vision became part of the cover artwork for Pazilat Omarova’s song Derkhab. Under the name Soldver PR, the artist helped bring the recording to digital music platforms. The song gained popularity organically and appeared in social media trends in 2024.

05

Cinematic research / 2024

An independent artistic interpretation of Corpse Bride and the development of original visual concepts extended the project’s exploration of cinema.
